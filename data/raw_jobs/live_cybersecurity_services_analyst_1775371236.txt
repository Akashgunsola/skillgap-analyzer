Job Title: Cybersecurity Services Analyst
Company: Venable LLP

Description:
Venable LLP’s Cybersecurity Group seeks a Cybersecurity Services Analyst to join the practice in the Washington, D.C. office.

Venable has the oldest and deepest privacy and cybersecurity practice in the country, serving as strategic advisors to clients in policy creation, high-stakes investigations, and litigation. The Cybersecurity Advisory arm was launched in 2015 under the leadership of former White House, NIST, and industry executives to augment the firm’s legal offerings with expertise in the technology, strategy, policy, business, and finance aspects of cybersecurity. Our “360 Degree Advisory Model” allows clients to depend on Venable for variety of services, including: legal advisory, privacy advisory, security services, organic growth strategy, business and market development, positioning and strategic communications, government engagement, and M&A advisory. More information is available at Venable.com.

In this role the Cybersecurity Services Analyst will be expected to operate successfully within a rapid paced and dynamic domain and be responsive to the requirements of multiple Directors, senior professionals, and clients. Time-management and organization must be adequate to meet the expectations of multiple projects and clients simultaneously.

Responsibilities
• Coordinate and track projects while keeping team members apprised of project status and deadlines
• Assist clients with cybersecurity risk assessment and incident response
• Conduct studies focused on bringing new offerings to the market
• Coordinate policy efforts with leading companies and government to drive better cybersecurity outcomes, and,
• Initially, the analyst will play a supporting role, but over time, opportunities to lead aspects of client engagement may arise

Qualifications
• Bachelor’s degree, is required
• Experience with, and an understanding of, the cybersecurity market and security risk management, is required
• 1 to 3 years of directly relevant professional and/or internship experience is preferred
• Multiple language skills is a plus
• Ability to work independently on multiple projects covering different aspects of our advisory model
• Manage rapidly-changing assignments
• Thrives in a fast-paced environment
• Ability to understand, assess, and articulate complex business, market, policy, and/or technical issues
• Effective interpersonal, written, and oral communication skills to work on case teams
• Excellent discipline and organizational, planning, analytical, and troubleshooting skills
• Ability to multitask and successfully manage a variety of demands daily from a variety of sources
• Strong attention to detail
• Ability to work a project independently and shows initiative in asking questions when needed
• Strong proficiency in MS Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint), is required
• Ability to handle sensitive matters and maintain confidentiality
• Meet the firm’s billable requirement, and,
• Committed to work outside of normal business hours and travel as needed, which may be extensive and include weekends or holidays

The anticipated base salary for this position is $65,000 per year. The actual salary offered will be based on a number of job related factors including, but not limited to, years of related experience, level of education, skills, credentials and certifications, responsibilities and performance.

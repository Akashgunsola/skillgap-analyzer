KPMG is seeking a talented Senior Associate, Cloud DevOps Engineering to enhance our Global Technology & Group capabilities. This role is crucial for driving cloud solutions and supporting AI/ML initiatives.

Key Responsibilities:
• Develop and automate cloud solutions on Azure, GCP, and AWS using Infrastructure-as-Code (IaC) and automation tools, focusing on scalability and security.
• Support AI/ML initiatives by automating integrations for scalable AI workloads, collaborating closely with senior engineers on tools like Azure AI Foundry and Google ADK.
• Enhance automation frameworks for infrastructure deployments, testing, and monitoring; create and maintain GitHub Actions pipelines focused on reusable workflows and security gateways.
• Ensure compliance and security across deployments, assisting in authentication implementations as needed.
• Collaborate with team members to troubleshoot technical issues and contribute to the technical direction set by experienced staff.
• Stay updated on emerging cloud technologies and share innovative ideas for process improvements.
• Demonstrate integrity, professionalism, and personal responsibility in maintaining a respectful work environment.

Qualifications:
• At least three years of recent experience in IT cloud development and automation engineering.
• Bachelor's degree in computer science, engineering, or related field, or equivalent professional experience.
• Hands-on expertise with Azure, AWS, and GCP cloud solutions using IaC tools like Terraform or ARM/Bicep, and scripting for both Windows and Linux.
• Strong understanding of AI/ML concepts and MLOps.
• Experience building agentic flows with Azure AI Foundry, Microsoft Agentic Framework, LangChain/LangGraph, and Google ADK.
• Excellent troubleshooting and problem-solving abilities with a knack for prioritizing tasks in a dynamic environment.
• Authorization to work in the U.S. without future employment-based visa sponsorship.

Job Summary:

We are looking for a proactive and detail-oriented Senior Quality Assurance Engineer with a talent for breaking websites and a strong knack for ensuring high-quality standards in the production environment. The ideal candidate will be an integral part of our team, working closely with Developers, Product Managers, and other Quality Engineers to manage and execute test cases throughout the development cycle with the aid of automation frameworks.

Key Responsibilities:
• Collaborate with development teams to identify use cases and create comprehensive test cases.
• Execute tests, report results, and document necessary adjustments.
• Utilize existing automation frameworks to expand the automated test suite.
• Ensure efficient operation within the production environment through regular communication with team members.
• Participate in development cycles to promote high-quality outputs from the start.

Must-Have Skills:
• 5 years of Strong knowledge and hands-on experience with JAVA and SQL.
• Proven experience in using Selenium for test automation.
• Expertise in Linux/Unix environments.

Industry Experience:
• A minimum of 4 years in Quality Assurance testing, particularly within website application testing, is essential.
• Experience or familiarity with AWS technologies is beneficial, though not strictly required.

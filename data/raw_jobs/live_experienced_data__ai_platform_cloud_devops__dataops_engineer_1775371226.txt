Job Description
At Boeing, we innovate and collaborate to make the world a better place. We’re committed to fostering an environment for every teammate that’s welcoming, respectful and inclusive, with great opportunity for professional growth. Find your future with us.
At Boeing Digital Services, you’ll be a part of a team that creates innovative digital solutions and analytics that drive the future and evolution of Digital Services and enable our customers to transform the way they do business. Using agile digital technology, our solutions optimize all aspects of the aircraft operations and maintenance ecosystem including safety, environmental sustainability, and efficiency.

As an Experienced Data & AI Platform Cloud DevOps / DataOps Engineer (Level 3), you will work as an DevOps with limited supervision to build and maintain our cloud environments, data pipelines, and manage Databricks in an Azure Cloud Platform. This position requires working closely with a team to ensure high availability and redundancy of all systems. The position will involve improving our build and data pipelines and ensuring successful delivery of new releases, developing infrastructure code, SRE-related tasks, and ultimately increasing value to the customer. This individual should have a persistent hunger to learn and solve problems in the pursuit of perfection in Seattle, WA.

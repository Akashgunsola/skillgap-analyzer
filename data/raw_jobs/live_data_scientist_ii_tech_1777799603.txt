Position requires:

• Database query languages including SQL;

• Data visualization using tableau or similar BI tools like looker or amplitude or mixpanel;

• Performing scalable analysis using R or Python;

• Experimentation techniques & statistical analysis including A/B testing, descriptive statistics, correlation, or confidence intervals;

• Developing relevant metrics or KPIs to measure performance by product teams;

• Collaborate cross-functionally and communicate complex topics through powerpoint presentations and analysis reports to audiences with different backgrounds;

• Translating high-level organizational objectives into technical data science roadmaps and specific machine learning project requirements to ensure analytical outputs directly support business goals;

• Big data frameworks (e.g., Spark and Hive) for scalable data processing.

Job Title: Quality Assurance Automation Engineer
Company: MyArmenianNetwork

Description:
Our client is an AI technology company building data-driven solutions for fintech and lending businesses, focused on real-time decisioning and predictive analytics.

As a QA Automation Specialist, your goal is to improve testing speed, efficiency, and coverage by automating repetitive and complex test scenarios.

Responsibilities

* Design, build, and maintain automated test scripts
* Ensure test coverage is reliable, reusable, and scalable
* Build, Maintain, and enhance existing automation frameworks
* Execute automated test suites and analyze results

* Debug and optimize automation scripts
* Identify, log, and track defects
* Report on automation coverage, effectiveness, and risks
* Collaborate with developers to identify automation opportunities

* Support performance, load, and stress testing
* Continuously improve automation processes and tools
* Stay current with automation trends and best practices

Requirements

* 3–5 years of QA experience, including test automation
* Strong programming skills
* JavaScript / TypeScript preferred
* Java, C#, or Python also acceptable

* Experience with Cypress, Playwright, Selenium, or similar
* Familiarity with CI/CD tools: GitHub Actions, Jenkins
* Experience with Git and version control
* Solid understanding of web technologies and databases

* Experience with test automation frameworks
* Exposure to Agile/Scrum environments
* Strong problem-solving and analytical skills
* Good communication skills (especially written English)

Nice to Have:

* Experience with BDD/TDD
* Performance testing tools (JMeter, Gatling)
* Multi-platform testing (web, mobile, desktop)

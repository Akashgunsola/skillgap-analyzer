Job Title: Cybersecurity Risk Analyst
Company: VirtualVocations

Description:
A company is looking for a Cybersecurity Risk Analyst to support enterprise-wide information security risk assessments.

Key Responsibilities :

Support and execute annual information security risk assessments across systems, applications, and business processes

Conduct stakeholder interviews and lead evidence collection and risk analysis activities

Prepare and maintain risk documentation, reports, and executive-level summaries

Required Qualifications :

5-7 years of experience in Cybersecurity, Information Security, or IT Risk Management

Proven experience performing information security risk assessments and risk analysis

Strong knowledge of security frameworks and standards (NIST 800-30, NIST CSF, COBIT, FFIEC)

Bachelor's degree in Information Security, Computer Science, Information Systems, or a related field

Experience in the financial services industry (preferred)
